Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions regarding our products, stock update, and counter collection feature.

How does the side-based local pickup(Click & Collect) work?

Select your item online across any department (clothing, home essentials, or accessories). During checkout, choose local pickup or standard delivery. Your order will be Completed at our main physical counter within 24 hours. You will receive an SMS code when yur pickup package is locked and ready or out for delivery.

Can i buy items from different departments in a single purchase?

Yes, Our online store is a universal cart and checkout system. This allows you to purchase kitchen towels,kids pyjamas,pots and denim jean together in one payment transaction saving you the shipping fees.

How accurate is the per-size item availability tracker?

The system connects directly to our in-store live point of sale retail system database, offering real-time stock checks for all sizes and variants. If an item option displayed as visible online, it is available on our physical warehouse shelves.
